👑 Ludo King

Rules

Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of the yard. A 6, a capture or a pawn reaching home gives another roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn. Landing on an opponent outside a star or start square sends it back to its yard. Pawns need an exact roll to finish. The game ends when all but one player have finished.
